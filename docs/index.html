Sudokube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Sudokube is developed by PAGI Studio. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Android application. No PAGI Studio account is required to play.

Data stored on your device

Saved games, gameplay settings, Premium entitlement status, streaks and game statistics are stored locally on your device. Statistics may include game mode, difficulty, completion or abandonment, completion time, mistakes, hints and continues. This data restores games and displays your progress. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Firebase Analytics

Sudokube uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and improve gameplay. It may process app launches, sessions, session duration, app interactions, app version, operating system, device model, language, approximate geographic region, in-app purchases and an identifier linked to the app installation. Advertising-personalization signals from Firebase Analytics are disabled by default.

Firebase Crashlytics

Sudokube uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose and fix crashes, non-fatal errors and application-not-responding events. Crash reports may include stack traces, error messages, app and operating-system versions, device model and state, installation identifiers and recent Analytics events used as diagnostic breadcrumbs. PAGI Studio does not attach names, email addresses or proprietary account identifiers to these reports.

Advertising and consent

The Free edition uses Google AdMob rewarded and interstitial advertising. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose an ad-supported action. Interstitial ads may appear between games or at other natural transitions, never while you are actively solving a puzzle. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address, approximate location inferred from the network, app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform requests consent before advertising is initialized. Available advertising choices can be reviewed under Settings → Manage Privacy Choices.

Purchasing Premium stops Sudokube from requesting advertisements. It does not disable Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. PAGI Studio does not receive or store payment-card details. The app stores the Premium entitlement locally and may ask Google Play to verify or restore the purchase.

Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games identity and gameplay data to provide sign-in, achievements and game statistics. Gameplay events may include game mode, difficulty, puzzle difficulty score, clue count, duration, completion or abandonment, mistakes, hints, continues and game overs. Puzzle contents and generation seeds are not sent.

Data sharing and international processing

PAGI Studio does not sell your personal data. Data described above is sent only to Google services used to provide analytics, diagnostics, advertising, billing and Play Games functionality. Google may process data on infrastructure located outside your country under its applicable terms and privacy safeguards.

Retention and your choices

Local data remains on your device until you reset it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Google retains Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising, billing and Play Games data according to the settings and retention periods applicable to each service. You can manage advertising consent in the app, manage Google Play Games from your Google account and use Android settings to reset or delete advertising identifiers.
