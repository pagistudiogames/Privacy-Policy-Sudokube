Sudokube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Sudokube is developed by PAGI Studio. No user account is currently required to play.

Advertising and consent

The Free edition uses Google AdMob rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose an ad-supported action. Interstitial ads may appear after a completed game, never during an active puzzle. Google's User Messaging Platform manages consent and available privacy choices.

Data stored on your device

Saved games, gameplay settings and game statistics are stored locally on your device. Statistics may include game mode, difficulty, completion status, completion time and mistakes. This data is used to restore games and display your progress.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is processed by Google Play. PAGI Studio does not receive or store payment card details. The app stores the Premium entitlement locally and may ask Google Play to restore it.

Google Play Games

When you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app may send gameplay events to Google to provide achievements and game statistics. These events may include game mode, difficulty, puzzle difficulty score, clue count, duration, completion or abandonment, mistakes, hints and continues. Puzzle contents and generation seeds are not sent.

Google processes this information according to its Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and related data through your Google account and Google Play Games settings.

Personal data

PAGI Studio does not sell your personal data. Google may process advertising and device information according to its own policies when AdMob is enabled, and gameplay information when Google Play Games is used.
