Sudokube Privacy Policy

Sudokube is developed by PAGI Studio. No user account is currently required to play.

Advertising and consent

The Free edition uses Google AdMob rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose an ad-supported action. Interstitial ads may appear after a completed game, never during an active puzzle. Google's User Messaging Platform manages consent and available privacy choices.

Data stored on your device

Saved games, gameplay settings and game statistics are stored locally on your device. Statistics may include game mode, difficulty, completion status, completion time and mistakes. This data is used to restore games and display your progress.

Personal data

PAGI Studio does not sell your personal data. Google may process advertising and device information according to its own policies when AdMob is enabled.
